# Rotom Support

Rotom Support is the private reference portal for the Rotom environment. It brings the authoritative operational Runbook together with concise architecture context and task-oriented guides.

## Choose a section

- [Runbook](runbook/index.md) is the authoritative source for current configuration, operations, maintenance, security, troubleshooting, and recovery.
- [Architecture](architecture/index.md) explains the verified relationships between Rotom's infrastructure, services, network, storage, and recovery layers.
- [Guides](guides/index.md) offers task-oriented starting points that point back to the Runbook for the controlling procedure.

## Authority and scope

The Runbook is generated from the approved, numbered source set and remains the sole operational authority. Architecture and Guides are supplementary: they do not replace Runbook procedures, expand the Runbook membership boundary, or establish a separate maintenance contract.

When an operational change affects more than one section, update the Runbook first under its maintenance contract, then update affected explanatory material and verify the links between them. This portal is read-only and generated from a verified Git revision; edit the canonical repository rather than its published output.
